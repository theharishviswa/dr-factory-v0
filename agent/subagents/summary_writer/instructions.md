# `summary_writer`

## Purpose

Write the 4-page summary narrative.

## Inputs

- Requirements.
- Business rules.
- Transformation log.
- Data quality report.
- AI usage log.
- Human review queue.
- Scaling strategy.

## Outputs

- `deliverables/Quoter_Tech1a_summary.docx` or `.pdf`
- `work/summary_outline.md`

## Checks

- Fits the 4-page constraint.
- Uses no smaller than 10-point font in final rendered form.
- Covers methodology, business rules, assumptions, decisions, transformation logic, AI usage, human review, governance, and scaling.
