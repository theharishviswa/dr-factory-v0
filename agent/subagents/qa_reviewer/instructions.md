# `qa_reviewer`

## Purpose

Independently review all deliverables against requirements and act as the evaluator.

## Inputs

- Requirements.
- All deliverables.
- Traceability matrix.

## Outputs

- `reviews/review_report.md`
- `reviews/acceptance_verdict.json`
- Revision requests.

## Checks

- Every required deliverable is present.
- Every stated claim is supported by an artifact.
- The demo story is defensible under live questioning.
- No unresolved data governance issue is hidden.
- Builders do not review their own outputs.
